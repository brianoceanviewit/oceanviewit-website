About

Built for businesses that outgrew guesswork.

Ocean View I.T. is built around one idea: small and mid-size businesses deserve the same level of technical depth that big companies get from a full in-house IT department — without carrying that overhead.

We handle the day-to-day: email, security, licensing, and support. And because our background is running IT at the director level for larger operations, every client gets that same experience built into the plan — not a subcontracted tech reading from a script.

Every password, license key, and credential we manage on your behalf lives in an encrypted vault — never a spreadsheet, never a sticky note. It's the kind of operational discipline you'd expect from a full IT department, run at small-business scale.
